# Backlinks and listings

A backlink is a link to your site from another site. It cannot be created from inside this website: it has to be earned or placed on
sites you control or have a relationship with. This is the plan, in the order that gives the most value for the least effort. Do not
buy links or join link schemes; they can get the site penalised.

## 1. Profiles you control (do these first)

Use the **same studio name, address and phone everywhere** (spelled and formatted identically). Always link back to the site.

| Profile | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | The main local listing; needs a real address or service area and phone |
| Bing Places | Same details for Bing |
| Facebook page, Instagram, LinkedIn page | Brand profiles; put the site link in the bio |
| Pinterest and YouTube | Interior projects are visual; link each pin or video to the matching project or service page |
| Houzz and Behance (portfolio sites) | Project galleries that link to the site |

After you create them, add the profile URLs to `site.social` in `src/config/site.ts`: they appear in the footer and in the
structured data (`sameAs`).

## 2. Relationships you already have

* Suppliers, brands and contractors you work with: ask for a "Our partners" or "Projects by" link.
* Clients who are happy to be named, and developers or architects you have worked with: a testimonial or credit that links to your site.
* Associations or chambers you belong to: member directory entries.

## 3. Content other people want to link to

* A finished project with real photos, the materials used and the cost range (your calculator page is a useful tool to link to).
* A short guide, for example "How to plan a kitchen budget", published on the site and shared in local groups.
* Local press and blogs: send finished projects with photos.

## 4. Inside the site (already done)

Internal links: header and footer link to every service; service pages link to related services and projects; the calculator and
consultation form are one click from every page. Photo credits link to the original authors.

## 5. Tracking

* Add the site to Google Search Console and check Links, Top linking sites, once a month.
* Keep a simple sheet: where you asked, who replied, link live or not.
* Ignore tools that promise hundreds of links for a fee.
